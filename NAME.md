# The name

Insider Threat Event Requirements (ITER) is the name of a work by Alessandro Aledda, published under CC BY 4.0. The content is free to use, change, and build on with attribution; the name identifies this work and its author.

## Using the name

Write **Insider Threat Event Requirements (ITER)** in full the first time it appears, in documentation, on a website, in a repository, in a publication, in a presentation, in training material, and in anything describing a product. ITER on its own is fine afterwards, where the context carries it. Do not alter the name, and do not set it in a way that suggests a different author or a different source.

## What using it does not mean

Using the record, its identifiers, its content, or anything mapped to it does not make the resulting work sponsored, endorsed, approved, certified, or supervised by Alessandro Aledda, and does not make it affiliated with him. Do not say or imply otherwise unless he has agreed to it in writing.

## Names of things you build

Do not take the name, or a name close enough to be confused with it, into the name of a product, a company, a service, a certification, a package, a repository, a domain, or an account, where doing so could leave anyone mistaken about who wrote or stands behind the thing. Describing what your work does is a different matter, and is welcome where it is true and does not imply endorsement: "mapped to ITER", "compatible with Insider Threat Event Requirements".

## Independence

ITER is written in a personal capacity. It is tied to no employer, no vendor, and no product, and nothing in it describes the internal practice of any organization its author has worked for. Contributing to it, mapping to it, implementing it, or building on it gives no editorial control over what it says and is not an endorsement of any product, service, or organization.
