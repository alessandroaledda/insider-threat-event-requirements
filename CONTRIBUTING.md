# Contributing

The record grows by contribution and by argument. A term may be stated
wrongly; a jurisdiction may have said something this does not record; a
reporting address may have moved; a source that belongs here may be missing.
All four are worth an issue.

## The one rule everything else follows from

**Nothing enters that has not been read.**

Not summarized by a search engine, not recalled from practice, not taken from
a secondary account. Read. The rest of what follows is how that is checked.

## Proposing a source

Open an issue, or a pull request against `library.md`, carrying:

- the **exact title**, as published, and in the original language where that
  is not English;
- the **issuer**, the **jurisdiction**, and the **date** the document carries;
- the **address** you opened, on the official or primary host. If the only
  copy that resolves is a mirror, say so;
- its **kind**: an act, a decided case, a register, guidance, an
  announcement, or a channel. The kind decides the evidence every row citing
  it will carry, and how long it may go unread;
- what it **establishes**, in one sentence, stated as a fact about the source
  and not as advice to a reader;
- a short **verbatim passage** carrying the point, with the article,
  paragraph, section, or page it comes from, in the original language, with an
  English rendering if the original is not English.

That last item is the check. A provision and its location cannot be produced
without having been inside the document.

## Proposing an act, a body, or a national act

A block in `iter.md` is a heading and `Key: value` lines. `SCHEMA.md` says
what every key means. An act under an instrument needs at least `Do`, `Owed
to`, `Term`, `From`, `Source` and `Read`; the build stops on one that lacks
them.

`Term`, `At` and `From` are the instrument's words. Nothing is computed from
them except the position of an act on the timeline, and a computed position
says so. Where a national act moves a term or the point it runs from, the row
says which, and quotes the wording it reads that from.

What a source states goes in `Implication`. What you conclude from it goes in
`Reading`, and the page marks it as a reading. A sentence that mixes the two
is split.

## Proposing a decided case

A case says who decided it, the facts, what was held, what followed as three
answers (documented, authority told, people told), what would have changed
the outcome, and which limb of which type it decides. A case whose actor is
external calibrates the limb and not the actor, and says so.

## Register

- American spelling. The Oxford comma.
- No em dashes.
- Short declarative sentences, in the third person. Nothing addresses the
  reader.
- No adjectives of praise, no marketing language, no *robust*,
  *comprehensive*, or *best practice* unless you are quoting.
- No ranking of sources, and no ordering by how useful something is.

Contributors are named unless they ask not to be.
